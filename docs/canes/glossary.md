# Canes workflow glossary

These definitions describe the accepted product decisions for Sebastian's requested changes. They do not claim that the corresponding storage or behavior is implemented.

| Term                     | Meaning                                                                                                                                                        |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Estimate                 | A customer quote for specified services and prices. It may be revised, sent, accepted, declined, or archived.                                                  |
| Estimate revision        | A version of a quote. Editing current work does not rewrite evidence of a previously accepted version.                                                         |
| Customer acceptance      | Approval recorded with the customer's drawn signature, printed name, agreement checkbox, timestamp, and the exact document and terms accepted.                 |
| Owner-recorded agreement | Sebastian records that the customer agreed by phone or in person. This is labeled separately from a customer signature.                                        |
| Signed snapshot          | Preserved evidence of the document and terms accepted at a particular time. Later revisions do not alter it.                                                   |
| Work order               | One occurrence of work for a customer. It has service lines, a status, and, when scheduled, an Eastern date and time.                                          |
| Service line             | Priced work, with quantity, price, and an optional fixed-dollar or percentage discount applied to the line subtotal before tax.                                |
| Checklist step           | A procedural crew task. It is distinct from the priced services shown to the owner or customer.                                                                |
| Repeat schedule          | An automation that creates scheduled work orders at a chosen cadence and Eastern date/time. It does not require a new signed agreement.                        |
| Recurring agreement      | An optional customer contract associated with repeated work. Existing signed agreements keep their history and terms.                                          |
| Visit                    | One work order generated by a repeat schedule. Edits default to this visit, with a separate this-and-future option. Skipping a visit does not stop the series. |
| Invoice                  | The bill for work. Its current amount and remaining balance must agree with its active payment link.                                                           |
| Requested deposit        | The amount the customer is asked to pay before the remaining balance. It is not evidence that money was received.                                              |
| Collected deposit        | An actual recorded payment allocated to the work. It reduces the remaining balance without reducing the original service price.                                |
| Payment                  | Money actually received from a customer. Revising or archiving a document preserves that transaction history.                                                  |
| Refund                   | Money returned to the customer, recorded as a separate financial event. A price edit does not itself send a refund.                                            |
| Overpayment              | Recorded net payments exceed the revised amount owed. It requires an explicit refund or credit decision.                                                       |
| Balance due              | The remaining amount the customer owes after applicable actual payments and refunds.                                                                           |
| Archive                  | Remove a record from normal working lists while preserving its business history.                                                                               |
| Delete                   | Permanently remove an unused draft that has no business history requiring retention.                                                                           |
| Cancel                   | Stop a visit or other future obligation. It is distinct from hiding a record and may require retiring a payment link.                                          |
| Recurring expense rule   | The amount, recurring date, and lifecycle controls used to create dated business expenses.                                                                     |
| Expense occurrence       | One dated business expense generated from a recurring rule or entered manually. Earlier occurrences survive later rule changes.                                |
| Employee payment         | Money Sebastian has already paid an employee outside Urso. It records amount, date, method, and optional note.                                                 |
| Calculated compensation  | An estimate of pay from hours, rates, or profit shares. It is not an employee payment.                                                                         |
| Business time            | America/New_York, independent of the phone's timezone.                                                                                                         |
| Calendar date            | A date without a time or UTC offset, such as a recurring expense date.                                                                                         |
| Appointment instant      | A scheduled point in time, stored with an offset/UTC representation and presented in Eastern time.                                                             |
| Calendar block           | A non-job hold on the schedule (time off, blocked time, holiday, or note). Distinct from a work order and from a quote visit.                                  |
| Uncontacted              | A `meta_ads` lead Sebastian has not yet called or texted from Urso, including ones he typed by hand. Not the same as a missed inbound call or a customer who texted last. |
| Unanswered inbound       | A missed call or a customer text waiting on a reply. Inbox orange. Distinct from uncontacted.                                                                  |
| Meta lead ingest         | Creating a Canes lead from a Facebook Instant Form with source `meta_ads`, instead of typing the Ads Manager row by hand.                                      |
| Contacted from Urso      | Sebastian sent an SMS or started a click-to-call (or logged a call outcome) from the app. That is what turns an uncontacted Meta row white.                    |
